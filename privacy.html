Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy covers "The Hidden Wiring Pipeline", a private automation tool used solely by the operator of the YouTube channel The Hidden Wiring to publish and evaluate the channel's own videos. The tool is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the channel operator signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

With the channel operator's own Google account authorization, the tool uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to the operator's own channel and update their title, description, privacy status and playlist,
  • read the operator's own channel information and playlists,
  • read aggregated statistics for the operator's own channel and videos (for example views, watch time and click-through rate).

The tool does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or any other third party. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individual viewers.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and are only used by the tool's automated workflows. Aggregated channel statistics are kept in that private repository for internal reporting. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
